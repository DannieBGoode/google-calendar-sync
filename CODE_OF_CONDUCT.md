# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, respectful feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what benefits the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language, personal attacks, trolling, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers through the contact method in the repository metadata. Maintainers will investigate fairly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
